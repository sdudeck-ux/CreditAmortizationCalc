Understanding Amortization & Strategies

What is Amortization?

Amortization is the process of spreading out a loan into a series of equal scheduled payments over time. Even though your total scheduled payment stays identical each month, the portion going toward interest vs. principal changes dramatically over time.

Principal The actual money you borrowed. Paying principal reduces your outstanding loan balance.
Interest The fee charged by the lender for borrowing money. It is calculated based on your remaining loan balance each period.
Bi-Weekly Payment Acceleration Paying half of your monthly payment every 2 weeks results in 26 half-payments a year (13 full monthly payments). The extra 13th payment directly reduces your loan principal!
Lump Sum & Extra Payments Any additional dollar paid over the required payment goes 100% to principal reduction, shortening the total term and slashing interest charges.
Key Takeaway: Making extra principal payments early in the loan has a far greater impact on total interest saved than making extra payments near the end of the loan!
