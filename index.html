Understanding Amortization

What is Amortization?

Amortization is the process of spreading out a loan into a series of equal payments over time. Even though your total monthly payment stays identical month after month, the portion going toward interest vs. loan principal changes dramatically over the lifetime of the loan.

Principal The actual money you borrowed. Paying principal reduces your outstanding loan balance.
Interest The fee charged by the lender for letting you borrow their money. It is calculated based on your remaining loan balance each month.
APR (Annual Percentage Rate) The annual interest rate charged for borrowing, expressed as a single percentage rate per year.
Bi-Weekly Payments Paying half your monthly payment every 2 weeks results in 26 half-payments per year (13 full monthly payments), shaving years off your loan!
Classroom Key Takeaway: When taking out long-term debt (like a 30-year home mortgage), you often pay more in total interest than the original loan amount itself during the first 15 years! Making extra payments early directly attacks the principal, drastically slashing total interest paid.
